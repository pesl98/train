Each unit has three deliverables

1 · NotebookTheory from first principles: every step is written out with its reason, followed by worked examples and quick "check yourself" questions that explain the answer.
2 · ProblemsA graded set at the end of each notebook. Your first attempt counts; then progressive hints and a full worked solution for every problem. 80 % first time = done.
3 · ExperimentPredict → run → verify. You write what you expect before running a simulator, then check it against the maths.
RulePaper first. Read with a pencil, predict before you run, and log every mistake in one line. The simulators test your understanding; they do not replace the derivation.
